## 2. Structured Items for Management Evaluation

### 2.1 Academic Review Items (Neha Patil — Founder & Academic Director)

#### Item ACAD-08-01: Discovery Session Workflow & Evidentiary Model
- **Proposed Architectural Decision:**  
  Approve the 12-stage Discovery Session workflow (`WS-08.1`) and the 7-stage evidentiary transformation pipeline (`WS-08.3`), strictly enforcing the non-diagnostic educational boundary and isolating Teacher Observations (`[OBS-TCH]`), Parent Context (`[CTX-PAR]`), and Child Voice (`[VOX-STU]`).
- **Pedagogical Rationale:**  
  Protects children from testing intimidation and clinical labeling; ensures that all recommendations given to parents are anchored in observable facts rather than subjective impressions.
- **Management Sign-off Status:** `[X] APPROVED   [ ] APPROVED WITH AMENDMENTS   [ ] REJECTED`
- **Signatory:** Neha Patil (Founder & Academic Director) — 04 November 2026

#### Item ACAD-08-02: Six-Dimensional Observation Rubrics & Qualitative Bands
- **Proposed Architectural Decision:**  
  Approve the behavioral observation rubrics across the six approved dimensions (*Number Sense, Arithmetic Understanding, Logical Approach, Learning Attitude, Response & Engagement, Interest & Learning Passion*) and the proposed 4-band qualitative scale (*Exploring, Developing, Secure, Extending*).
- **Pedagogical Rationale:**  
  Provides mentors with an objective framework to observe thinking habits without assigning pass/fail scores or calculating artificial child IQ metrics.
- **Management Sign-off Status:** `[X] APPROVED   [ ] APPROVED WITH AMENDMENTS   [ ] REJECTED`
- **Signatory:** Neha Patil (Founder & Academic Director) — 04 November 2026

#### Item ACAD-08-03: Multi-Factorial Age-Nine Programme Crossover Framework
- **Proposed Architectural Decision:**  
  Approve the 6-criteria diagnostic evaluation matrix and 5 legitimate pedagogical recommendation pathways for children aged 8.5 to 10 evaluating Abacus vs. Vedic Math vs. School Math vs. IPM/Olympiad.
- **Pedagogical Rationale:**  
  Replaces rigid chronological age cutoffs with holistic developmental readiness (concrete bead preference vs. abstract pattern stamina).
- **Management Sign-off Status:** `[X] APPROVED   [ ] APPROVED WITH AMENDMENTS   [ ] REJECTED`
- **Signatory:** Neha Patil (Founder & Academic Director) — 04 November 2026

#### Item ACAD-08-04: Parent Feedback Document Architecture
- **Proposed Architectural Decision:**  
  Approve the 8-section layout and parent-friendly communication tone of the official written feedback deliverable: **`AAA Demo Observation & Learning Recommendation`**.
- **Pedagogical Rationale:**  
  Empowers parents with positive, actionable guidance while transparently disclosing that a single 60-minute session is an observation of current comfort, not a permanent ceiling.
