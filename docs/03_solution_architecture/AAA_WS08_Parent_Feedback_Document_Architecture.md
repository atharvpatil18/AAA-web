```
"Simple, Stress-Free Home Encouragement Tips:
1. Praise the Effort, Not the Speed: Celebrate your child's persistence and thinking pauses. Speed will emerge naturally from conceptual confidence.
2. Play 'Friends of 10': During daily routines (car rides, dinner), play quick guessing games (e.g. 'I have 7, who is my friend to make 10?').
3. Keep School Math Low-Stress: Avoid giving timed test drills at home. Allow math to feel like an enjoyable puzzle."
```
