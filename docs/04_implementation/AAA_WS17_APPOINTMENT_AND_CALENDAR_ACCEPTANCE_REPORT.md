## 1. Executive Summary & Audit Scope

This report audits the Tier 2 Appointment Confirmation experience and the RFC 5545 calendar invitation generator (`calendarInviteGenerator.ts`). The objective is to verify that appointment confirmation is strictly decoupled from initial inquiry ingestion, that event identity and sequence incrementation prevent calendar duplicates, and that the invitation strictly conforms to international calendaring standards.
