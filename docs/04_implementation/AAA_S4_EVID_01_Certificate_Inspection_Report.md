## 4. Key Evidentiary Findings & Legal Constraints

1. **Absence of "Master Trainer" Title:**
   - Neither certificate uses the word "Master". Both explicitly award the exact qualification:
     - *"Certified Trainer of Abacus"*
     - *"Certified Trainer of Vedic Maths"*
   - **Directive:** The title "Master Trainer" cannot be used on the public website based on these documents. Public wording must strictly adopt **"Certified Trainer"**.
2. **Centre Name Divergence:**
   - Certificate 1 explicitly names **"Atharv Vedic Math & Arnav Abacus Academy, Wakad Pune"**.
   - Certificate 2 names **"Atharv Vedic Math, Pune"**.
   - **Directive:** AAA can state it is an Authorised Skill Centre in Abacus (`CI2023/100000455`). For Vedic Mathematics, Neha Patil is individually a "Certified Trainer of Vedic Maths" (`CI2023/100000403`); however, AAA cannot claim that Arnav Abacus Academy is a separately licensed Vedic Maths centre without cross-authorization documentation.
3. **No Independent Third-Party Trademark License:**
   - The presence of the Skill India, NSDC, and ISO 9001:2015 marks on AVAS's certificate template establishes that AVAS holds these approvals. It does **not** grant AAA an independent license to publish standalone Skill India, NSDC, or ISO logos on its website.
