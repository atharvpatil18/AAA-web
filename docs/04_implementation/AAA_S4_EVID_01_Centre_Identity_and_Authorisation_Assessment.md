### 2.2 The Vedic Mathematics Centre Authorization (`CI2023/100000403`)

- **Missing Trade Name:** The trade name *"Arnav Abacus Academy"* does **not** appear on this certificate.
- **Analysis:**

- **Regulatory Risk:** Claiming publicly that *"Arnav Abacus Academy is an Authorised Skill Centre in Vedic Maths"* without documenting the legal cross-licensing or unified proprietorship between Atharv Vedic Math and Arnav Abacus Academy could be challenged as inaccurate.
