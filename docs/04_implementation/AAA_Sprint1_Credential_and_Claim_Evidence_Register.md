## 2. Definitive Legal & Marketing Attribution Rules

With this primary documentary evidence in hand, the earlier ambiguity regarding Skill India, NSDC, and ISO 9001:2015 is resolved:

### 2.1 What is FULLY SUBSTANTIATED and Legally Defensible:
1. **Authorised Skill Centre Ownership:**  
   Neha Patil and Arnav Abacus Academy are legally authorized to state:
   - *"Authorised Owner of a Skill Centre in Abacus & Vedic Maths (AVAS / Skill India Approved Training Partner Network)"*
   - Citing registration numbers: `CIAVAS/CI2023/100000455` and `CIAVAS/CI2023/100000403`.
2. **Skill India / NSDC Approved Partner Network:**  
   Because AVAS is an official Approved Training Partner of NSDC and Skill India, AAA operating as an authorized center under AVAS is fully entitled to reference:
   - *"AVAS Certified Skill Centre • NSDC & Skill India Approved Training Partner Network"*.
3. **ISO 9001:2015 Quality Standards:**  
   The certificate explicitly bears the **ISO 9001:2015** certification seal and IAF accreditation mark issued to the skilling network. AAA may legitimately state:
   - *"ISO 9001:2015 Quality Standards Aligned"*.
4. **Trainer Titles:**  
   - *"Certified Trainer of Abacus"*  
   - *"Certified Trainer of Vedic Maths"*  
   - *"Master of Computer Applications (MCA)"*

### 2.2 What Remains Correctly Prohibited:
- **"Government Recognized" / "सरकारी मान्यता प्राप्त":**  
  Skill India / NSDC partner networks are skilling initiatives and public-private partnerships, not Central/State Government formal educational recognitions. The permanent removal of "Government Recognized" from `Footer.tsx` remains strictly enforced.
- **State Emblem of India (Ashoka Lion Capital):**  
  Must NEVER be used. The certificates use the official Skill India and NSDC corporate logos, which are fully permitted under the authorized training network.
