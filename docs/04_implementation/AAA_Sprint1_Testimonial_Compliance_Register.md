## 1. Compliance Standard & Regulatory Requirements

Under Section 6 of the CCPA Guidelines for Prevention of Misleading Advertisement in Coaching Sector, 2024:
1. **Verifiable Authorship:** Every testimonial must represent an authentic student or parent with valid enrolment records.
2. **Mandatory Metadata:** Published representations must disclose the student’s identity, enrolled course, course duration, and whether the enrollment was paid or complimentary.
3. **Written Parental Consent:** Because students are minors (ages 4.5–14), written parental consent authorizing the publication of student names, feedback, or achievements must be maintained on record.
4. **No Misleading Generalization:** Academies must not claim or imply that individual success outcomes are typical, automatic, or guaranteed.
