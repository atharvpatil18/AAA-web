## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose

Under management determination `MGT-DET-S4-PE-04` and the Workstream 4 authorization (`AAA-S4-WS-PROG-01`), this matrix establishes the formal pedagogical, curricular, and evidence baseline for the five core educational programmes offered at Arnav Abacus Academy (AAA), Wakad, Pune:

1. **Abacus Mathematics**
2. **Vedic Mathematics**
3. **Mental Mathematics**
4. **School Mathematics**
5. **IPM & Olympiad Mathematics**
