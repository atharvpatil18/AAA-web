## 1. Executive Summary & Brand Governance Principle

Under Indian trademark law, consumer protection regulations, and institutional brand identity guidelines, possessing a certificate issued by an approved partner does **not** automatically grant a downstream local center the right to reproduce or advertise third-party registered trademarks, government program logos, or international standards marks on its commercial marketing portal.

This register establishes the permissible and prohibited usage for every mark associated with AAA's educational operations.

## 3. Mandatory Website Trademark Directives

1. **Zero Third-Party Logos on Commercial Web:**  
   No standalone image files, SVGs, or marketing badges representing Skill India, NSDC, ISO 9001:2015, or government departments shall be uploaded or rendered on the public website.
2. **Textual Attribution Discipline:**  
   All references to skilling affiliations must be framed factually and descriptively, citing the exact certificate numbers on record.
3. **Audit Compliance:**  
   Any external audit under CCPA or consumer protection authorities will find that AAA makes zero unauthorized trademark reproductions, upholding the highest standards of regulatory integrity.
