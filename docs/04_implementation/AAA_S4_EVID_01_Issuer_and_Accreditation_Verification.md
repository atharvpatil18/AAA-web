## 2. Institutional Accreditation Disaggregation & Legal Standing

### 2.1 Skill India & National Skill Development Corporation (NSDC)
- **Primary Partner:** **CLS Future Edutech Private Limited (AVAS)** is the formal "Approved Training Partner" of NSDC / Skill India.
- **Arnav Abacus Academy Standing:** AAA is an **authorized local skill centre** established under Neha Nitin Patil's entrepreneurship certification from AVAS (`CI2023/100000455`).
- **Legal Limitation:** AAA is **not** a direct primary contracting partner of the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship (MSDE) or NSDC. Its affiliation is derivative through AVAS.
- **Permissible Claim:**  
  *"Authorised Skill Centre under AVAS — Skill India & NSDC Approved Training Partner Network"* (Citing Reg. No. `CIAVAS/CI2023/100000455`).
- **Prohibited Claim:**  
  Claiming that AAA is an independent, direct "Skill India Partner" or using standalone government-style badges implying direct Ministry accreditation.

---

### 2.2 ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management System Certification
- **Mark on Certificate:** The certificates feature the **AB CB** and **IAF (International Accreditation Forum)** marks alongside **ISO 9001:2015**.
- **Underlying Certificate Holder:** The ISO 9001:2015 certification belongs to the franchisor/parent entity: **CLS Future Edutech Private Limited (AVAS)** for its curriculum development and training processes.
- **Arnav Abacus Academy Standing:** AAA does not hold an independent ISO 9001:2015 certificate issued directly to its Wakad premises.
- **Permissible Claim:**  
  *"Curriculum developed under ISO 9001:2015 certified training processes (AVAS network)"*.
- **Prohibited Claim:**  
  Claiming that Arnav Abacus Academy itself is "ISO 9001:2015 Certified" or displaying standalone ISO seals on the website.

---

### 2.3 Indian Institute of Vedic Maths & Abacus (IIVA)
- **Mark on Certificate:** Circular seal reading "IIVA" (with Devanagari script).
- **Scope of Endorsement:** IIVA Delhi provides syllabus benchmarks and competition endorsements for AVAS championships and trainer skilling.
- **Permissible Claim:**  
  *"Curriculum incorporating IIVA and AVAS speed arithmetic techniques"*.
- **Prohibited Claim:**  
  Claiming that AAA is a direct corporate subsidiary of IIVA or claiming a "Master Trainer" title without separate documentation.
