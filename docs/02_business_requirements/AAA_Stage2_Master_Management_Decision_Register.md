## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose

This register provides the definitive, single-source-of-truth log of all **fifty-four (54) strategic and operational decisions** formulated across the five Stage 2 business requirements workstreams (WS-01 through WS-05) and formally ratified by AAA Management (**Neha Patil** and **Nitin Patil**).

### Consolidation Principles:
1. **Zero Silent Re-openings:** No ratified decision has been altered, diluted, or re-opened during consolidation.
2. **Preservation of Qualifications:** Every condition, deferral, limitation, and pilot caveat is preserved with exact wording.
3. **Strict Governance Integrity:** Conditional approvals are strictly distinguished from unconditional approvals, and proposed strategies are distinguished from operational facts.
4. **Implementation Boundary:** Decision approval ratifies business requirements only; it does not authorize code changes or live deployments.
