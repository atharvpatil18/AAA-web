### 3.3 Active Academic Streams `[MANAGEMENT CONFIRMED]`
AAA offers five specialized child-development and foundational mathematics streams:
1. **Soroban Abacus Mathematics:** Primary cognitive focus for ages 4.5 to 9 years (8 progressive levels).
2. **Vedic Mathematics:** High-speed calculation shortcuts based on the 16 sutras for ages 9 to 14 years.
3. **Mental Mathematics:** Numerical agility, place-value intuition, and rapid recall for ages 6 to 14 years.
4. **School Mathematics Foundation:** Conceptual mastery and board rubric alignment for Grades 1 to 10 (CBSE, ICSE, State Board).
5. **IPM & Olympiad Mathematics:** Higher-Order Thinking Skills (HOTS) and competition problem solving for Grades 2 to 9.
