## 3. Detailed Decision Records

### DEC-WS03-01: Parent Persona Framework

* **Decision Statement:** Whether to adopt the four behavioral parent personas—(1) The Foundation Builder, (2) The Anxious Remediator, (3) The Academic Enhancer, and (4) The Competitive Aspirant—as the strategic segmentation model for AAA parent guides, website pathways, and admissions workflows.
* **Recommendation:** Adopt the four personas as qualitative behavioral segments. Explicitly preserve that personas represent fluid orientations rather than permanent parent identities, and a single parent may exhibit characteristics of multiple personas. Age ranges are indicative guidelines marked `[PENDING VALIDATION]`. Prohibit asserting unverified statistics or survey percentages.
* **Evidence & Assumptions:**
  - Qualitative operational observations by Neha Patil and Nitin Patil handling parent inquiries in Wakad and PCMC schools.
  - Demographic profiles of Wakad schools (Wisdom World, EuroSchool, Akshara, Mount Litera Zee School).
  - Assumption: Wakad parents prioritize foundational understanding and cognitive confidence over rote coaching.
* **Dependencies:** Timetable and curriculum audit confirming program age structures and stream entry prerequisites.
* **Approval Status:** **MANAGEMENT APPROVED**
* **Approval Scope & Qualifications:** Approved. Adopt the four behavioural personas as flexible, overlapping segments. Indicative age ranges remain pending validation.
* **Management Comments:** "Approved. Adopt the four behavioural personas as flexible, overlapping segments. Indicative age ranges remain pending validation."
* **Date of Approval:** 02 November 2026
* **Implementation Boundary:** Requirements approval is not implementation approval. No website copy changes or digital marketing campaigns authorized.

### DEC-WS03-02: Eight-Stage Customer Journey Architecture

* **Decision Statement:** Whether to formalize and adopt the sequential eight-stage parent customer journey—from initial digital discovery through classroom onboarding, progress reviews, and voluntary community advocacy—as the baseline operational flow for AAA's digital presence and physical center operations.
* **Recommendation:** Adopt the sequential 8-stage journey: (1) Discovery, (2) Enquiry, (3) AAA Personalised Learning Discovery Session (45–90 min free demo), (4) Upfront Feedback & Recommendation (11-point sheet), (5) Admission & Onboarding, (6) Structured Learning & Progress (max 5 students/batch), (7) Parent Engagement, Retention & Renewal, and (8) Advocacy & Referrals. Each stage documents role allocation between Neha Patil and Nitin Patil, emotional states, touchpoints, friction points, and success metrics.
* **Evidence & Assumptions:**
  - Synthesizes approved DEC-WS02-08 (Discovery Session name), DEC-WS02-09 (11-point Feedback Sheet), and DEC-WS02-13 (micro-batch maximum of 5 students).
  - Assumption: Structured division of administrative triage and academic leadership delivers high parent satisfaction while protecting teaching focus.
* **Dependencies:** Physical center timetable audit and batch availability alignment.
* **Approval Status:** **MANAGEMENT APPROVED**
* **Approval Scope & Qualifications:** Approved. Adopt the eight-stage journey from Discovery through Advocacy & Referrals. The role allocation between Neha Patil and Nitin Patil is an operating proposal and does not independently constitute approval of permanent delegation.
* **Management Comments:** "Approved. Adopt the eight-stage journey from Discovery through Advocacy & Referrals. The role allocation between Neha Patil and Nitin Patil is an operating proposal and does not independently constitute approval of permanent delegation."
* **Date of Approval:** 02 November 2026
* **Implementation Boundary:** Requirements approval is not implementation approval. No production routes or CRM systems authorized.

### DEC-WS03-03: Enquiry Response Standard

* **Decision Statement:** Whether to establish a target inquiry response standard and, if so, what operational window and delivery structure should be adopted for WhatsApp and website lead handling.
* **Recommendation:** Adopt **Option A as a proposed pilot target**, not an unconditional permanent SLA: Immediate automated WhatsApp acknowledgment upon submission, followed by personal human follow-up within 2 operating hours. Explicitly preserve that founder classroom teaching takes absolute priority over marketing response speed, transparent expectation notices must cover weekends and center rest days, and official operating hours remain `[PENDING VALIDATION]`.
* **Evidence & Assumptions:**
  - Inquiries arrive via WhatsApp and web lead forms to Nitin Patil (`+91 90219 24968`).
  - Wakad parents frequently research multiple enrichment options simultaneously; prompt acknowledgment prevents inquiry drop-off.
  - Assumption: Automated acknowledgment reassures parents instantly, enabling human follow-up within 2 operating hours.
* **Dependencies:** Validation of official administrative operating hours; setup of WhatsApp automated acknowledgment in WS-05 / WS-06.
* **Approval Status:** **MANAGEMENT APPROVED AS PILOT TARGET ONLY**
* **Approval Scope & Qualifications:** Approved as a pilot target only. Immediate automated acknowledgement and human follow-up within two operating hours are proposed pilot standards. Actual operating hours, pilot duration, workload and performance must be validated before permanent adoption.

### DEC-WS03-04: Minimum Necessary Data Privacy Principle

* **Decision Statement:** Whether to adopt the "Minimum Necessary Data Privacy Principle" across all parent inquiry channels, lead generation forms, physical registers, and analytics tracking, replacing prior references to "zero-PII booking".
* **Recommendation:** Formally adopt the principle: *"AAA collects only the minimum personal information necessary to respond to the parent, arrange the demo, and manage the admission enquiry, with appropriate consent, access control, and privacy safeguards."* Embed the 8 specific privacy facets: (1) Purpose limitation, (2) Data minimisation, (3) Informed consent, (4) Role-based access control, (5) Retention and deletion rules, (6) Child data protection (zero sensitive personal data), (7) Non-disclosure / zero commercial sharing, and (8) Zero-PII analytics telemetry (GA4 `G-M2EL9MYSRL`). Note clearly that this is the governance framework for future implementation, not a claim of current complete technical automation.
* **Evidence & Assumptions:**
  - Parents are willing to provide essential contact and child grade details to book a demo, provided data is handled respectfully and securely.
  - Compliance with ethical child protection standards and statutory data privacy principles in India (DPDP Act alignment).
* **Dependencies:** Technical design of privacy policies, consent checkboxes, and lead capture forms in WS-05 / WS-06.
* **Approval Status:** **MANAGEMENT APPROVED AS BUSINESS REQUIREMENTS PRINCIPLE**
* **Approval Scope & Qualifications:** Approved as a business requirements principle. Detailed privacy procedures, consent workflows, retention/deletion rules, access controls and technical enforcement remain subject to subsequent design, validation and implementation approval.
* **Management Comments:** "Approved as a business requirements principle. Detailed privacy procedures, consent workflows, retention/deletion rules, access controls and technical enforcement remain subject to subsequent design, validation and implementation approval."
* **Date of Approval:** 02 November 2026
* **Implementation Boundary:** Requirements approval is not implementation approval. No privacy policy publication or form schema modifications authorized.

### DEC-WS03-05: Progress Communication & Retention Model

* **Decision Statement:** Whether to adopt a sustainable 4-tier communication model for student progress tracking and parent retention that celebrates individual growth without administrative overwhelm for teaching staff.
* **Recommendation:** Adopt the 4-tier progress communication framework: (1) Informal weekly pickup touchpoints (zero paperwork), (2) Level completion milestone reviews (formal evaluation certificate), (3) Bi-monthly qualitative progress chats (~10 min per child, once every two months), and (4) Annual academic pathway consultations. Strictly evaluate child progress against their own previous baseline; prohibit public ranking boards or competitive shaming. Mark mentor workload and scheduling feasibility as `[PENDING VALIDATION]`.
* **Evidence & Assumptions:**
  - High parent retention depends on transparent visibility into child progress.
  - Neha Patil's primary focus must remain classroom pedagogy; excessive reporting paperwork would degrade teaching quality.
* **Dependencies:** Timetable audit and active student census to confirm scheduled consultation windows and tutor capacity.
* **Approval Status:** **MANAGEMENT APPROVED AS PROPOSED FRAMEWORK**
* **Approval Scope & Qualifications:** Approved as a proposed framework, subject to operational capacity validation. Clarify that bi-monthly means once every two months. Certificates recognise programme milestones and must not imply guaranteed developmental outcomes.
* **Management Comments:** "Approved as a proposed framework, subject to operational capacity validation. Clarify that bi-monthly means once every two months. Certificates recognise programme milestones and must not imply guaranteed developmental outcomes."
* **Date of Approval:** 02 November 2026
* **Implementation Boundary:** Requirements approval is not implementation approval. No student portal features, digital report cards, or administrative software authorized.

### DEC-WS03-06: Ethical Advocacy & Referral Model

* **Decision Statement:** Whether to establish formal ethical policies governing parent reviews, referral invitations, and student achievement showcases on the AAA website and marketing materials.
* **Recommendation:** Anchor academy advocacy in authentic parent satisfaction: (1) Voluntary Google Reviews leveraging the existing verified 74+ five-star baseline, (2) Organic word-of-mouth referrals across local school networks, and (3) Documented student achievement showcases on `/showcase` requiring explicit written parental consent. Strictly prohibit review incentives, discounts, gifts, coercive review gating, or public disclosure of child contact details.
* **Evidence & Assumptions:**
  - AAA currently holds 74+ genuine five-star Google Reviews reflecting authentic parental appreciation.
  - Coercive review gating or paid testimonials undermine premium academic trust in close-knit school communities.
* **Dependencies:** Documented parental consent template and showcase intake form in WS-04 / WS-05.
* **Approval Status:** **MANAGEMENT APPROVED**
* **Approval Scope & Qualifications:** Approved. Reviews and referrals must remain voluntary and non-incentivised. Student showcases require appropriate documented parental consent.
* **Management Comments:** "Approved. Reviews and referrals must remain voluntary and non-incentivised. Student showcases require appropriate documented parental consent."
* **Date of Approval:** 02 November 2026
* **Implementation Boundary:** Requirements approval is not implementation approval. No website showcase updates or review solicitation campaigns authorized.
