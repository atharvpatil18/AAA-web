## 1. Executive Summary & Messaging Principles

This document provides the structured messaging hierarchy, copywriting guidelines, and narrative frameworks for AAA’s digital front door, admissions consultations, and reception touchpoints.

### Core Governance Copy Rules:
1. **Accurate Batch Sizing:** Always use the exact approved expression: *"Personalised learning in small batches, with a maximum of five students per batch."* (Never use "Guaranteed 1:5 ratio" or imply uninterrupted one-to-one teaching).
2. **Certified Educators:** Always use *"Guided by certified educators with a focus on structured learning and individual student progress."*
3. **No Unaudited Test Claims:** Completely removed references to having "passed the 5-Second Parent Reassurance test." All messaging is classified as a proposed communication direction requiring future parent validation.
4. **Verified Review Proof Point:** Always cite *"Trusted by families, with 74+ five-star Google Reviews"* with the required reference to the live Google Business Profile audit (verified November 2026).
5. **Positive Self-Positioning:** Strictly position AAA on its own strengths; zero disparaging comparisons to competitor class sizes.
6. **No Unsupported Outcomes:** Refined all headlines to focus on process-oriented language (structured mathematics learning, personalised guidance, and individual progress) rather than promising lifelong results or guaranteed cognitive improvements.

## 5. Parent Counselling & Admission Conversation Narratives `[PROPOSED STRATEGY]`

### 5.1 Parent Counselling Narrative (Neha Patil & Nitin Patil)
* **Opening Reassurance (First 5 Minutes):**  
  *"Welcome to Arnav Abacus Academy! We are so glad you brought [Child's Name] today. As parents, it's completely natural to worry when a child counts on fingers, makes careless slips, or feels nervous about math tests. But we want you to know: calculation hesitation is rarely a lack of intelligence. It simply means abstract numbers haven't been given a tangible, visual meaning yet. In today's discovery session, we are not testing or grading your child; we are simply observing how they approach numbers and where their confidence begins."*
* **The Personalised Demo Transition:**  
  *"We will spend the next 45 to 60 minutes exploring hands-on Soroban abacus beads and fun arithmetic patterns together. We'll observe their number sense, logical approach, and engagement in a stress-free, encouraging environment."*
* **Upfront Parent Feedback Transition:**  
  *"Right after the session, we will sit down together and share our detailed observations—highlighting your child's natural strengths, areas where support will help, and our concrete learning recommendation."*
