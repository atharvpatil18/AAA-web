## 2. Operational Baseline Confirmations (Management Confirmed)

1. **Discovery Session Operating Practice & Model:** Confirmed. The existing operational delivery of a free, unhurried discovery session lasting approximately **45 to 90 minutes** (adapting dynamically to child comfort and parent discussion) is preserved. The role division (Neha Patil: academic observation, educational feedback, programme recommendation; Nitin Patil: intake coordination, parent orientation, fee information, operational logistics) is confirmed as a **proposed operating model**, preserving historical delivery flexibility without narrowing who conducts sessions.
2. **The Six Approved Educational Observation Dimensions:** Confirmed. Observation during discovery sessions strictly evaluates the **exact six approved formal names**:
   1. *Number Sense*
   2. *Arithmetic Understanding*
   3. *Logical Approach*
   4. *Learning Attitude*
   5. *Response and Engagement*
   6. *Interest and Learning Passion*
3. **The Eight Approved Child Development Pillars:** Confirmed. Marketing copy and pedagogical frameworks must strictly preserve the exact WS-04 names:
   1. *Visualization*
   2. *Working Memory*
   3. *Pattern Recognition*
   4. *Processing Speed*
   5. *Attention*
   6. *Cognitive Flexibility*
   7. *Executive Function*
   8. *Confidence*
4. **Minimum-Necessary Data Standard & Privacy Safeguards:** Confirmed. Inbound discovery web forms are restricted to 4 core fields: Parent Name (required), Mobile/WhatsApp (required), Child Age Bracket OR School Grade (one required, not both), and Programme of Primary Interest (optional). Separate consent is required for inquiry response vs. promotional updates (declining promotional updates must never block enquiry submission). Exact child DOB, school name, residential street address, and clinical checkboxes are strictly banned at inquiry. The proposed 90-day retention schedule remains unapproved pending legal review (`ACT-WS05-05`). Privacy controls represent Stage 3/4 design requirements and are not currently technically implemented in code.
5. **Refined Admissions Funnel Lifecycle & Counting Criteria:** Confirmed. The admissions lifecycle standardizes on eight distinct statuses:
   `New Enquiry` $\to$ `Contacted` $\to$ `Discovery Session Booked` $\to$ `Discovery Session Attended` $\to$ [`Admission Completed` | `Decision Pending` | `Closed – Not Proceeding` | `Follow-up Later`].  
   **Strict Admission Counting Rule:** `Admission Completed` is recorded **ONLY upon formal student registration and verified tuition fee payment** documented by an official fee receipt. Expressions of interest, verbal agreements, or discovery session attendance alone must never be counted as admissions.
6. **Tooling Strategy & Governance Gating:** Confirmed. Permanent commercial CRM selection is deferred. Interim spreadsheet or manual tracking protocols are **strictly gated behind the completion and sign-off of mandatory prerequisites `ACT-WS05-05` (privacy notice & retention review) and `ACT-WS05-06` (access control, 2FA, formula sanitization)**. WhatsApp remains a primary communication tool but must not be the sole, untracked store of admissions interactions.
7. **Hyper-Local Catchment Prioritization:** Confirmed. 0–3 km Wakad catchment is prioritized as an operational hypothesis and primary focus, not an exclusive boundary. Secondary PCMC catchments (Hinjawadi, Pimple Saudagar, Tathawade) will be validated through empirical census data (`ACT-WS05-08`).
8. **Ethical Google Review Policy:** Confirmed. 100% voluntary, non-incentivized Google review collection policy. Zero fee discounts, vouchers, or gifts. Current 74+ review count treated as a periodically refreshable baseline via quarterly audit (`ACT-WS05-07`). The **48-hour response turnaround is a proposed operational target pending management confirmation**, ensuring that no published response discloses personal identifiable information of students or parents (zero-PII rule).
9. **Elimination of Speed Hyperbole:** Confirmed. Universal elimination of unsupported speed claims ("10x to 15x") across all digital assets; replaced in Stage 4 by exam verification speed, mental calculation poise, and procedural error reduction.
10. **Inquiry Response Operating Window:** The two-operating-hour inquiry follow-up standard is approved as a pilot principle. The proposed operational window (**Monday–Saturday, 9:30 AM – 7:30 PM**) is **pending management confirmation (`EVD-MKT-04`)**; the live operating window must be formally declared and confirmed before the pilot commences. Founder classroom teaching hours are strictly preserved as top priority over inquiry follow-up.
11. **KPI Dashboard Math & Baseline Logging:** Confirmed. 17-metric admissions KPI dictionary and 6-metric weekly MVD approved. Ratio metrics define explicit numerators/denominators with `N/A` for zero denominator. Numerical conversion targets are deferred until an empirical **four-week baseline collection period** is completed post-implementation under approved registers.
